1. What is the purpose of a color map?
    The purpose of a color map is to add the flat colors of the texture.
2. What does a normal map simulate?
    The normal map simulates different heights in the surface of the texture without adding any polygons.
3. How does a roughness map affect lighting?
    The roughness map dictates which parts of the texture reflect lighting really well and which ones not so much.
4. Which texture improved realism the most and why?
    I think the floor texture improved realism the most because it had the most widespread effect and impacted the most surface area.
5. What advantages do texture maps have over adding more geometry?
    Texture maps are easier to line up since you apply them to the existing geometry, whereas adding more geometry you have to carefully align it with the existing geometry.
